About The Academy

Where Dedication Meets Direction

Established in Mohanpuri, Meerut, Prashasti IAS Academy is a premier coaching institute that provides top-notch guidance to students preparing for the UPSC Civil Services Examination and UP-PCS. Our mission is to give aspirants the right direction, structured study material, and expert mentorship — helping them turn their dream of becoming an IAS/IPS/PCS officer into reality.
